Option A · Recommended

Keep it left, tighten everything

Title stays where it is (good for SEO h1 and page identity) but drops to 20px, the subtitle sits inline next to it, vertical padding is halved, and the sidebar top offset is removed so Featured starts right away. Smallest change, biggest space win.
